About CITY-MOVE

CITY-MOVE studies how cities can accelerate, support and evaluate actions for active movement for health. It follows 13 physical activity actions in six cities on three continents: Antwerp, Bogotá, Kampala, Lima, Ljubljana and Rotterdam.

My role

I work on the data side of the project: what cities already collect about the places where people walk, play and cycle, and what that routine data can show. First I mapped the routine data available in the six partner cities. Now I rebuild those data for every home, to measure what residents have within reach, how it changes over time, and which changes can be studied as natural experiments.
